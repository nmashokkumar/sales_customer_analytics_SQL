# 📊 Sales & Customer Analytics | Business-Driven SQL Analysis in Retail

## 📌 Executive Summary

This project analyzes sales, customer, and product data using SQL to answer critical business questions that support revenue growth, customer retention, and product strategy. A star schema data model was validated to ensure reliable business reporting and scalable analytics.

- **🎯 Business Problem:** Identify revenue drivers, high-value customers, and product performance using transactional sales data.
- **⚙️ Approach:** Built a star schema, validated data quality, and answered business-driven SQL questions using joins, aggregations, CTEs, and analytical queries.
- **📈 Impact:** Generated insights across **customer behavior, revenue trends, product performance, and data quality** through **20+ SQL business analyses**.

---

# 💼 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate thousands of transactions daily, but raw transactional data alone cannot support strategic decisions. Business leaders need accurate insights into revenue trends, customer value, and product performance to optimize growth.

This project transforms raw sales data into actionable business intelligence by validating the data model and answering practical business questions that support informed decision-making.

---

# 🛠️ Methodology

- 🗄️ Designed and populated a **Star Schema** consisting of Fact and Dimension tables using SQL.
- 🔍 Validated primary keys, referential integrity, business rules, and revenue calculations.
- 📊 Performed business-driven analysis on sales, customers, and products using SQL.
- 🔗 Combined multiple dimension tables through optimized joins for analytical reporting.
- 📈 Evaluated revenue trends, customer purchasing behavior, and product performance using aggregated business metrics.

# 📈 Results & Business Recommendations

The analysis provides business-focused insights into revenue generation, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and data quality. These findings can support better commercial planning, inventory optimization, and customer retention strategies.

### 🔍 Key Insights

- 💰 Identified total revenue and total units sold across the business.
- 📅 Revealed monthly revenue trends to identify growth and seasonal patterns.
- 🏆 Identified the highest revenue-generating products and categories.
- 👥 Ranked high-value customers based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) metrics.
- 💲 Distinguished revenue growth driven by pricing versus sales volume.
- ✅ Validated primary keys, referential integrity, revenue calculations, and business rules.
- 🔗 Confirmed star schema joins preserved data integrity without duplicate records.

### 💡 Business Recommendations

- 🎯 Prioritize inventory and promotions for consistently high-performing products.
- ❤️ Develop loyalty programs targeting high-value customers identified through RFM analysis.
- 📉 Investigate months with declining revenue to uncover operational or seasonal factors.
- 💵 Monitor pricing and sales volume together to optimize pricing strategy.
- 🛡️ Automate data validation checks before loading new transactional data into reporting systems.
- 📊 Expand the dimensional model with region, sales channel, and promotional data for deeper business insights.
